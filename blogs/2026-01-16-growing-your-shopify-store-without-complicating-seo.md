If you run a Shopify store, you already know SEO matters — even if you don’t think about it every day. What often does come up, though, is concern around growth: “If I add more products or use an app, am I going to mess up what already works?” That’s a very real worry, especially in jewelry, where catalogs can get complex fast. It’s also why Nivoda Connect is designed to work naturally within Shopify, instead of forcing merchants into a separate system.
